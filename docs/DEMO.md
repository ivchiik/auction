# Hiring pitch walkthrough

Use this after the native acceptance checks pass. The app is called Auction.

## Two-minute story

1. Open on Play: "The same social auction, designed for a phone." Show the typography, daily table and five topics.
2. Open Sports, then Soccer. The Starting XI leads: $40 for eleven picks, one goalkeeper through one striker, with attackers, midfielders and defenders below and a switch to all-time greats. Pick a mode and configure You versus The Shark. Explain that friends can also share one phone with up to four seats.
3. Start the draft. Make one bid and one pass. Show how the budget cap protects enough money to finish the roster, with essential controls within thumb reach.
4. Open rosters. Names and prices are visible; ratings remain hidden. Leave and resume to demonstrate native autosave.
5. Finish a prepared draft, compare the unrevealed rosters, then reveal the verdict. Show the winner, each roster's average rating and the steal; open the share sheet and cancel.
6. Show the five daily challenges and Collection. Explain that this prototype runs locally and can play without a backend.

## What the prototype demonstrates

Product interpretation, original mobile design, a deterministic auction engine, three computer personalities, local recovery, native interaction and verification on both target platforms. Refer to QA.md for actual results; never claim a check that remains pending.

## Conversation with the company

Confirm the final brand, content refresh/export contract, priority for full catalog and additional game modes, their preferred support/privacy details, signing/developer accounts and release timeline. The mobile daily challenge is a local prototype sequence until their exact seed contract is shared. No account, online multiplayer or production synchronization is implied.
