# Paddle — creative brief

Working name shortlist: Paddle (auction paddle; approachable), Last Bid (dramatic, descriptive), Draftclub (social), Going Once (auction phrase). Paddle is the temporary implementation name; availability/trademark clearance has not been assessed.

## Visual thesis
A beautiful game-night companion meets an independent sports/culture magazine. Warm ivory #F6F2E9, almost-black ink #1C2520, bold vermilion #F25736, dusty lavender #D8D0F0, acid yellow #E4EF85 and deep forest #193D31. Avoid generic casino chips, neon dashboards and excessive rounded cards.

Oversized confident serif headlines paired with tight, legible sans labels and tabular bid numerals. Generous whitespace. Fine ink rules, stamp-like topic labels, distinctive auction paddle mark and offset collage accents. Editorial illustrations/real content images give each topic character. Strong black/forest action surfaces. Small playful details, restrained motion.

## Native behavior
393×852 design baseline; also review 360dp and short screens. Content scrolls independently of fixed primary bid controls. 48–56pt touch areas. Safe area. Do not bury turn owner/budget. Active player has a color plus a text label. No reliance on color alone. Game card uses portrait art with readable name and metadata. Hidden rating badge reads Reveal later, not fake question-score data.

## Required screens for Brilliant
1. Home: Paddle wordmark, 'Good taste. Better bids.', daily hero, quick play and distinctive five-topic grid/list, restrained bottom navigation (Play/Daily/Collection).
2. Set the table: topic summary, 2/3/4 seat segmented control, player names and bot choices, clock toggle, $20/five picks summary, Start drafting fixed action.
3. Live auction: clear active seat/bankroll, large artwork/card, current bid, opponent status, reserve rule hint, bottom bid +1/+2/+3 and Pass controls, roster drawer affordance.
4. Verdict: reveal anticipation then winning composition, score comparison, drafted picks and best-buy/overpay, share/play again.
5. Daily: five daily challenges with local progress and a clear date, local streaks, no invented social stats.
6. Rules and roster drawer states if space allows.

## Voice
Short, witty and clear: 'Your move.', 'Make them overpay.', 'Going, going… yours.', 'Good eye.', 'The table has spoken.' Explain rules where an action is constrained. Never imply real-money wagering.

Deliver editable designs to this design folder and PNG exports. Check composition notes and fix overflow before sign-off. Implementation will follow these tokens and layouts with responsive native styling.
